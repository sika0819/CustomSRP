## Root cause (code + bake)
- Player uses CharacterController + NavMesh.SamplePosition XZ clamp (no NavMeshAgent).
- Bake agentRadius 0.5 << BodyRadius 1.1 → corridors that look blue can be too tight for the capsule.
- Clamp assigns transform.position while CC stays enabled → PhysX fight = severe jitter at edges/gaps.
- SamplePosition fail within maxDist → full horizontal rollback (binary stick).
